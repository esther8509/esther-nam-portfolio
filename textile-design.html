SCREENPRINTING

I loved to experiment with color and with graphics I drew with my own hands, and to create patterning.

Screenprint on cotton

JACQUARD WEAVING

A jacquard is a weaving machine that lets you manipulate patterns digitally and weave manually. I had a lot of fun weaving on it, and I experimented a lot with color and texture.

CROCHET

During COVID, I had the chance to collaborate with my mother on running an Etsy shop of household crochet items. I was in charge of designing the products, marketing, running the Etsy platform, social media, and photography, and my mom and I crocheted the products together. TikTok brought us the most organic sales, which was super cool to see. We had a ton of fun on this collaborative project.
