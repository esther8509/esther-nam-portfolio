Developer Tools

Compliance experiences on Apple's developer platform.

OVERVIEW

I'm currently designing compliance experiences on Developer Tools, Apple's developer platform for publishing, managing, and distributing apps across Apple platforms. My current focus: helping developers declare whether their apps use AI-generated content or offer encrypted messaging, so Apple can meet regional regulatory requirements.

Early framework for how I was mapping this problem out — comparing compliance obligations across two feature categories. Figures redacted.

AI-Generated Content Encrypted Messaging
App Features in Scope Apps with AI generated content Apps with end-to-end encrypted messaging
Apple Obligation Enforce only regionally compliant apps with AI-generated content are available in that region's storefront Enforce only regionally compliant apps with encrypted messaging are available in that region's storefront
Developer Obligation Provide several docs to prove compliance to distribute in that region Provide doc to prove compliance to distribute in that region
