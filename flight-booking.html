Flight Booking

Redesigning the AlaskaAir.com booking experience, with accessibility as a first-class constraint, not an afterthought.

OVERVIEW

I redesigned the AlaskaAir.com booking experience across web and mobile, with a specific focus on accessibility, ensuring the flow held up for screen reader users, keyboard-only navigation, and a wide range of assistive technology, not just visually. That included a redesigned date picker with flexible dates, and 16 UI assets I designed and contributed to Alaska's Auro Design System, the shared component library now deployed across the airline's digital products, airport kiosks, and in-flight systems nationwide.

AUDIT

I ran an audit of the existing AlaskaAir.com booking experience. I annotated the booking widget and traced how the homepage had changed over time.
